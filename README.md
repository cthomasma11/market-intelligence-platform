# AI-Powered Market Intelligence & Investment Research Platform

Institutional-style financial intelligence and investment research infrastructure powered by AI, NLP, and quantitative analytics.

---

## Overview

This project is an AI-powered market intelligence platform designed to analyze financial market data, generate investment research insights, evaluate risk metrics, and automate investment memo generation.

The platform combines:

- Quantitative finance analytics
- NLP sentiment analysis
- Financial market data pipelines
- Automated research workflows
- Institutional-style dashboard infrastructure

---

## Features

### Market Intelligence Dashboard
- Real-time stock market data
- Portfolio analytics
- Volatility analysis
- Drawdown analysis
- Risk metrics

### AI/NLP Sentiment Engine
- Financial headline analysis
- Earnings commentary sentiment scoring
- Research text classification
- Confidence scoring

### Investment Memo Generator
- Automated research summaries
- AI-generated investment insights
- Risk assessment reporting
- Market performance analysis

---

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Streamlit
- Pandas
- Plotly
- Transformers (HuggingFace)
- yfinance
- NLP sentiment analysis
